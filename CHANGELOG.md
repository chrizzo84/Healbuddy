# Changelog

## v0.3.0

- Pin heal targets: a new click action ("Pin heal target", Alt + middle click
  by default) puts up to five players into a larger row at the top
- Rank ladder: the "Ladder" button in the bindings puts three ranks of a spell
  on one mouse button (click, Shift, Ctrl)
- Preview: show 5, 10, 25 or 40 example frames while adjusting the appearance
- Cooldown icons below the mana bar, selectable on the new "Cooldowns" page
- Penance, Holy Shock and Dispel Magic are now offered in the spell list

## v0.2.1

- First public version
- Click-casting on party and raid frames with spell and rank selection
- Mana bar with 5-second rule and regeneration ticks
- Buff and debuff icons, dispel border, aggro border
- Missing class buff indicator and "buff all" button
- Skins and appearance settings
- English and German localization
